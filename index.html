Academic methodology

Least Cost: produces a low-cost feasible starting allocation.
MODI: checks/improves transportation allocation using opportunity costs.
Simplex LP: represents the final allocation as a linear programming minimization problem.
Route layer: sequences the assigned customers for each vehicle and evaluates time, distance, fuel and CO₂.
